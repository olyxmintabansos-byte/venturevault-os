SUMMARY OF TERMS FOR SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK FINANCING

VentureVault Systems Inc.

This Term Sheet summarizes the principal terms with respect to the Series A Preferred Stock financing of VentureVault Systems Inc. by Horizon Horizon Capital Partners VII.

Issuer:VentureVault Systems Inc.
Lead Investor:Horizon Horizon Capital Partners VII
Securities:Series A Preferred
Investment Amount:$8,000,000
Pre-Money Valuation:$32,000,000
Post-Money Valuation:$40,000,000
Purchase Price Per Share:$3.2000 per share
Option Pool Reservation:10% unallocated post-closing pool for employee equity.
Liquidation Preference:1x Non-Participating (pari-passu with prior Seed Preferred).

1. Board of Directors Governance

The Board of Directors shall consist of five (5) members upon closing: 2 Founders (CEO & CTO), 1 Horizon Partner, 1 Mutual Independent Industry Expert.

2. Protective Provisions (Negative Covenants)

So long as at least 25% of Series A Preferred Stock remains outstanding, the Company shall not take any of the following actions without majority Series A consent:

  • Amendments to Certificate of Incorporation adversely affecting Series A
  • Creation or authorization of senior or pari-passu equity securities
  • Any M&A merger, asset sale, or liquidation event under $50M
  • Declaration or payment of any dividend on Common or Preferred stock

3. Vesting & Lock-Up

Common Stock held by Founders will be subject to a standard 48-month vesting schedule with a 12-month cliff. Standard double-trigger acceleration upon a Change of Control event shall apply.
